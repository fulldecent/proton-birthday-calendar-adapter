Proton Birthday Calendar Adapter

Turn the birthdays in your contacts into a calendar file you can import into Proton Calendar. Everything happens in your browser — your contacts are never uploaded anywhere.

  1. Export your contacts as CSV:
    • Google: open Google Contacts → Export → Google CSV
    • Outlook: open Outlook People → Manage contacts → Export contacts (help)
  2. Drop the CSV file below (or click to choose it).
  3. Download the calendar file and import it in Proton Calendar → Settings → Import.
